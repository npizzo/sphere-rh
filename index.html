REPRODUCTION SCOPE

The original random realization is not specified. Seeded runs follow the Figures 5–6 protocols; matching the published resolution is a separate requirement. They are not expected to trace the original coefficient curves point for point. Agreement is assessed through initial conditions, conservation, spectra, and pattern evolution.

INDEPENDENT COMPARISON

A double-precision spherical-harmonic Galerkin solver uses dealiased quadratic products and exact linear Rossby propagation, with fourth-order integration of nonlinear interactions. JAX enables CPU and GPU execution. Shared initial coefficients permit comparison with the stereographic Fortran method. Conservation of energy and enstrophy alone does not establish spatial convergence: finite harmonic truncation does not preserve every Euler Casimir, and unresolved enstrophy can accumulate near the cutoff. CONTEXT

An exact family, an open dynamical question.

For a streamfunction confined to degree n, vorticity is proportional to streamfunction: ζ = −n(n + 1)ψ on the unit sphere. Its nonlinear self-advection therefore vanishes. Rotational symmetry accounts for the harmonic degeneracy; this cancellation is the additional reason the entire multiplet gives exact nonlinear solutions.

On a solid-body background of angular speed Ω, the pattern speed is −2Ω/[n(n + 1)] in the co-rotating frame and Ω{1 − 2/[n(n + 1)]} in the inertial frame. For degree two these speeds are −Ω/3 and 2Ω/3, respectively. The proposed limit is a fixed degree-two projection, P₂ψ, in this prescribed moving frame, including its amplitude. The locked degree-one component is excluded; other degrees need not freeze.

A high degree-two energy fraction alone does not establish freezing of P₂ψ. Both normalized coherence and amplitude-inclusive degree-two profile distances test persistence after the prescribed rotation is removed. Angular momentum is monitored separately as a constraint; rotation-invariant shape is a separate check. The Figure 6 controls have Ω = 0, so their prescribed speed is zero. The Ω = 50 comparison uses the same initial spectrum to follow degree two in its prescribed moving frame. Statistical rotational symmetry would require a separate ensemble test.

Benchmarks: Salmon & Pizzo (2023), Figures 5–6. Geometric comparison: Salmon & Pizzo (2024), Two-dimensional turbulence on the ellipsoid.
